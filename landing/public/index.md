## Complete Money Toolkit for your app in minutes

Wallets, bridging, swaps, DCA, lending, and confidential transfers. One SDK, every money primitive your app needs.

- **Swap:** token swaps with best-price execution via AVNU and Ekubo
- **Lend and Borrow:** lending and borrowing via Vesu
- **Gasless Transactions:** via AVNU and Cartridge Paymaster
- **Social Login:** Google, Apple, and Passkeys via Privy and Cartridge
- **Bitcoin Staking:** BTC, USDC, and STRK yield
- **DCA:** dollar-cost averaging with scheduled recurring buys
- **Bridge:** cross-chain bridging via LayerZero, Hyperlane, and StarkGate
- **Confidential Transfers:** zero-knowledge proofs via Tongo.cash
- **Cross-platform:** Web, Android, and iOS

## What does shipping look like?

**With Starkzap** (`npm install starkzap`): ship in minutes, 10 / 10 features ready out of the box.

**Without Starkzap** (build it yourself): months of work, 3 / 10 features, with significant extra work.

| Feature | With Starkzap | Build it yourself |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wallets & Social Login | Yes | Partial: Privy + Cartridge integration |
| Gasless Transactions | Yes | Partial: custom paymaster setup |
| Bitcoin Staking | Yes | No: no unified solution |
| Token Swaps | Yes | Partial: AVNU or Ekubo integration |
| Cross-Chain Bridging | Yes | No: not available |
| Dollar-Cost Averaging | Yes | No: not available |
| Lending & Borrowing | Yes | Partial: Vesu integration needed |
| Confidential Transfers | Yes | No: not available |
| Web + Android + iOS | Yes | No: build per platform |
| No new language to learn | Yes | No: Cairo, Solidity, or Rust |

## FAQ

### Who can build on Starkzap?

Anyone with access to the internet can now ship products in minutes.

### Do my users need to know anything about web3?

Not at all. Starkzap abstracts away wallets, gas fees, and blockchain complexity. Your users get a familiar web2 experience while you get the benefits of onchain infrastructure.

### Can I bridge tokens from other chains?

Absolutely. Starkzap makes cross-chain bridging effortless. Move tokens from Ethereum, Solana, and other chains onto Starknet with just a few lines of code.

### How easy are swaps?

Insanely easy. One function call and you're done. Starkzap routes through AVNU and Ekubo to guarantee the best price every time. No manual DEX hunting required.
